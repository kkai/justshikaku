No account, no login, no demo credentials needed. Everything works offline and
all progress is stored on the device.

There is one non-consumable in-app purchase, de.kaikunze.shikaku.full, which
unlocks five of the seven technique lessons, the practice drills, the
escalating teaching hints, the 10x10 and 12x12 boards, and the full
statistics screen. Free play covers the rules tutorial, the first two
lessons, 5x5 and 7x7 boards at every difficulty, best times, and an error
check that flags a rectangle that cannot be part of the solution.

Two things that may look inconsistent but are deliberate:

1. Tapping a locked board size bounces the selection back and opens the
   purchase screen instead of disabling the chip, so the feature stays
   visible rather than hidden. The same applies to locked lesson rows.

2. A rectangle whose size disagrees with its number is allowed to stay on
   the board, marked with a red count. Free players' hints are errors-only,
   so mistakes must be possible to make; the hint then points at them.

Difficulty is never behind the purchase: a free player can play the hardest
tier on the free board sizes.
