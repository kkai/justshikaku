WHAT CHANGED SINCE THE PREVIOUS SUBMISSION

This build is a substantial revision responding to the earlier 4.3(a)
finding. The app's differentiator is functional: it is, to our knowledge,
the only Shikaku app with a named-technique curriculum. Concretely in this
build:

- Seven named solving techniques, each with an interactive lesson.
- A hint ladder that draws the solver's argument on the player's own board
  (candidates, eliminations, the deciding cell, dimension arrows, and the
  technique's name lettered above the grid). It never auto-solves; its last
  rung applies a single argued move.
- A curriculum-tied daily puzzle: each date's board is generated so a
  scheduled technique appears in its logical solution; unaided completion
  advances that technique's mastery.
- Mastery tracking verified against the engine: an unhinted correct
  rectangle is credited only when the logical solver can independently
  derive it from the position it was played in.
- Timed practice drills per technique.
- Every board is machine-verified before play to have exactly one solution
  AND to be solvable by the taught techniques alone, so guessing is never
  required.
- A post-solve replay that grades the player's own moves against the
  solver: worked out, laid by a hint, or beyond the curriculum's reach.
- Four ways to practise that are not timed: The Climb (rooms up the
  ladder, three strikes), Spot It (ten solver-verified positions where one
  technique is the move), per-technique timed drills, and a request for a
  whole board generated to contain a chosen technique.
- Today's room, a week view with a seal for a complete week, and every mode
  explained in Learn and behind an info button on its own screen.
- A distinct visual identity: dark, opaque, drawn digit glyphs, no themes.

PURCHASE MODEL

One non-consumable, de.kaikunze.shikaku.full. Free tier: rules tutorial,
two lessons, today's room with its streak and weekly seal, The Climb,
5x5/7x7 at every difficulty, best times, error check. The purchase adds the
other five lessons, drills and Spot It, the full hint ladder, 10x10/12x12,
and full statistics. Difficulty is never paywalled.

TWO DELIBERATE BEHAVIOURS

1. Locked sizes/lessons stay tappable and open the purchase sheet rather
   than being disabled, so what exists is visible.
2. A rectangle whose area disagrees with its number is allowed to commit,
   marked with a hatched overlay and a have/need count, because the free tier's
   errors-only hints need mistakes to be possible before they can point
   at them.
